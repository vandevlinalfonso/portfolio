01 · The System

Defense that never sleeps.

The machine behind this text is the gate every change must pass. Raw strands stream in from the void, chaotic and untested, and the aperture aligns them into one ordered braid. A flawed strand is severed at the rim before it ever enters. Nothing flawed ships.

That is the honest shape of DevSecOps. You do not chase a perfect, finished state: you build the machine that catches problems automatically, every commit, every day, so the humans can sleep.
